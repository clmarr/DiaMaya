# DiaMaya

## Transcription conventions

### Final vowels

Writing Early Hieroglyphic Maya as written, with vocalisms -- this will allow analyses htat have been made in scholarship a chance to emerge from the data on their own without assuming them a priori.

## Stages 

Proto-Cholan : ancestor of all the below.

Early Hieroglyphic Maya : circa 300 CE

West, East Hieroglyphic Maya : later regionalized varieties of HM. 

Later texts may show conservative forms but earlier texts will not show a sound change that has not yet happened.

Chontal -- colonial attestation in Acalan Chontal (c. 1600) and modern attestation.

Ch'orti' -- supposed descendant of East Hieroglyphic Maya
